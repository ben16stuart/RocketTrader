## 5. The honest finding — this was an infrastructure week, not an analysis week

Four defects, all previously known, all cost real money or real opportunity:

| # | Defect | Cost | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Whole-share-only orders** → ~14% permanent idle cash | **−0.49% this week**, the largest single loss | ✅ **FIXED today** |
| 2 | **Scanner `Change` column dead** — `+0.0%` every row, 5 sessions | `top_movers` was a ranked list of zeros; the both-list overlap tier produced nothing for 4 straight sessions | ✅ **FIXED today** |
| 3 | **Midday routine has not run since June 15** | CSTL's 2:32 PM stop-out went unseen for 90 minutes | 🔴 **Still broken — needs a decision** |
| 4 | **Rocket cannot measure its own return** | Every performance number in `portfolio_state.md` is wrong | 🔴 **Newly diagnosed** |

### Fix 1 — fractional orders (`scripts/alpaca_client.py`)
IWM at ~$300 against a ~$3,150 slice makes **one whole share 9.6% of the book against a
3% rebalance band** — the band was mathematically unsatisfiable, so the rebalance logic
correctly refused to trade and correctly left ~14% in cash, every session, forever.
Lesson 7a identified this on 7/28 and named the fix (fractional orders); it then sat open
for eight sessions while the drag compounded. `buy`/`sell` now accept fractional
quantities via a `_fmt_qty` helper (9dp, Alpaca's limit). Trailing stops **reject**
fractional qty with a loud `ValueError` rather than leaving a satellite silently
unprotected — only the stopless IWM core may be fractional.

### Fix 2 — scanner change column (`scripts/smallcap_scanner.py`)
Root cause found: **Finviz renamed the header `Change` → `Change %`.** The code did
`s.get("Change", "")`, got nothing, and `_safe_float("")` returned `0.0` — a broken
instrument rendering a plausible number, exactly lesson 14's failure mode. Now accepts
both spellings. Verified live: `top_movers` returns a real ranked board again.

**This also partly retires lesson 16.** The scanner's *prices* were fine — QNST $20.87,
STLN $6.39, RelVol 6.2x all check out against raw bars. The premarket "wrong price"
observation was extended-hours quoting, not corruption. **Lesson 16 was over-broad and
made Rocket distrust a working tool for a week.** Corrected in `lessons_learned.md`.

### Defect 3 — the midday routine (needs a human decision)
`com.benstuart.rocket.midday.plist` is **valid and on disk but absent from
`launchctl list`.** The last log is **2026-06-15**, and it ends with
`API Error: Request rejected (429) · rate limit`. So it did run historically, died on
quota, and never came back. **I have deliberately not reloaded it.** Bull and Rocket
already share one Claude quota across 10 launchd jobs, and lesson 12 records a session
limit costing Rocket the AMCX entry outright. Adding a fifth Rocket job would compete
directly with **premarket and market_open, which are the higher-value windows.**
**→ Escalated to the user: reload midday and accept more contention, or leave it off and
accept that intraday stop fills are discovered at EOD?**

### Defect 4 — Rocket's return figure is Bull's return (newly diagnosed)
`portfolio_snapshot.py` computes `rocket_return = (0.30 × shared_account_value) /
REBASE_ALLOCATED_VALUE − 1`. Rocket's slice is a **fixed 30% of the shared account**, so
this expression is *algebraically identical to the whole account's return* — **Bull's
P&L included.** The headline "Rocket +3.95% vs SPY +4.20%, −0.25%" does not describe
Rocket. It describes Bull-and-Rocket combined, and Bull holds 5 of the 6 positions.

Every weekly review since the merge has quoted this number. The hand-built attribution in
§1 (−0.54%) is the real figure; the reported one (−0.25%) is off by 29bp in Rocket's
favour, and the error will grow with Bull's book.

**Fix path** (next week's priority 1): the reconciler already knows which positions belong
to whom. Rocket's book = Σ(Rocket's position values) + Rocket's notional cash, which needs
a persisted cash ledger rather than being derived from a percentage of the shared account.
Not attempted today — it needs a real cash baseline, and a half-fix would be worse than a
known-broken number.

**RCEL — rejected for the wrong reason, and got lucky.** Friday's premarket eliminated it

on a genuine beat-and-raise (revenue $21.7M +18%, record 81.9% gross margin, FY guidance
raised to $86–89M).

**But it should have been skipped anyway** — for a reason Rocket never got to. RCEL has an
**active $200M S-3 shelf (31 Mar 2026), has already drawn on it, did a $15M Australian
placement, and has under a year of cash runway.** That is a live dilution overhang into
exactly the spike Rocket would have been buying — the SOC/SVCO/STLN pattern.

**The lesson is not "the ADV gate is too tight."** It is that **a borderline gate decided
on a stale number is not a real decision, and the right outcome via the wrong gate is
luck, not process.** Next time the near-miss name will be clean, and Rocket will reject it
by 5,000 shares of averaging noise.

## 7. Strategy changes made

- **Deployment is no longer "solved."** The core sleeve fixed *allocation* on 7/28 but
  whole-share granularity quietly re-created a 14% cash position. Fractional orders close
  it for real. Monday's `market_close` must execute the first fractional top-up.
- **New tiebreaker: close-position-in-range.** With a board full of real catalysts, where
  a stock closes in its daily range separated the survivors from the corpses better than
  catalyst quality did. Below the day's midpoint on heavy volume = distribution = dead.
- **Analyst price targets on a print day are stale by construction** — treat a breached
  target as a prompt to check for revisions, not as a disqualification.
- **Retired the blanket distrust of scanner prices** (lesson 16 was over-broad).
