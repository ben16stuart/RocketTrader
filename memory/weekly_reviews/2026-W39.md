## 6. The finding that matters — the floor as specified can't be met at this size

**The satellite floor is unreachable unless all four slots are full at max size, all the time.**

- The sizing formula gives 1.5% risk ÷ 7% stop = **21.4% of slice**, which the 15% cap then
  binds. **Every satellite is sized at exactly 15%.**
- 3 satellites = **45% → still a breach.** Only **4 of 4 slots, each at full size and not
  down more than ~12% combined**, clear 50%.
- Satellites hold 1–5 days, and new positions are capped at 5 per week. **Keeping 4 slots
  continuously full needs ~4–5 entries a week, every week.** That is the entire weekly
  allowance, on a funnel that has produced **1 entry in the last 19 sessions** (RARE,
  W36–W39; OMER on 8/14 was the one before it).
- So the floor does not work as a forcing function at the margin. It reads "breached"
  on almost every session regardless of effort, and daily alarms that always fire carry no
  information (the lesson 46g/51e objection).

**This is a CLAUDE.md guardrail, so it is escalated, not changed.** Options for Ben:
(a) the floor counts *open slots* (e.g. ≥2 of 4) rather than % of slice;
(b) lower the floor to ~30% (two max-size satellites);
(c) raise max satellite size to ~25% so 2 positions clear 50%; this raises single-name risk.
Rocket's recommendation is **(a) or (b)**: keep the pressure on stock-picking, but make it
reachable in a normal week.

**Second, related finding: gate accretion.** `lessons_learned.md` had grown to **69
numbered lessons and ~99KB (≈25k tokens loaded every session)**. Almost every lesson since
W35 adds a reason to reject a name. Only two (5g, 54c) ever made a pass more likely.
The kill checks are individually defensible and were often validated against the bars.
But a funnel that only ever tightens will reach zero throughput, and Rocket's has. The
file was compressed this session (Step 7b). Rocket also commits to a rule going forward:
**a new lesson must either replace an existing check or state what it lets through.**
Documenting tool defects doesn't count as a trading rule and goes in the instrument
section.
