### AGEN — BUY 47 @ $9.81 (9/29) → stopped 47 @ $9.56 (10/01 9:49 ET), −$11.75 / −2.5%

1. **Catalyst real?** ✅ Rodman & Renshaw initiation, Buy/$19, dated 9/28. Day-1 close was
   +10.4% at 77% of range on 3.6× median volume, a real reaction. It was the weakest
   catalyst type on the hierarchy (rung 6), taken under rule 8's MEDIUM bar.
2. **Timing?** ✅ This was the best-executed entry in weeks. The log names the prior
   settled close ($9.435, rule 70 ✓). The 9:30–9:45 range was 3.9% (2a ✓) and the
   9:45–9:50 bar closed in its upper half (2b ✓). Rocket waited for that bar rather than
   buying the open.
3. **Exit?** ✅ The trailing stop did its job. AGEN closed 10/01 at **$8.33 (−16.1% on
   the day, low $8.28)**. Against that close, the $9.56 fill saved **≈$57**. It still
   closed 10/02 at $8.36. No discretionary error.
4. **What was ignored?** The bear case was written down at entry: **~55M deep-ITM PIPE
   warrants, resale-registered**. An analyst initiation doesn't take that supply away.
   The 10/01 collapse came with no headline, which is what selling from that overhang
   would look like (unconfirmed). **Rule 8 lowered the catalyst bar, and the trade lost
   on the dilution dimension (rule 8 of the lessons file).** That isn't a reason to undo
   the MEDIUM bar. It does mean that when the bar is MEDIUM, the dilution grade should
   carry more weight.
